Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: August 1, 2026 App Store Billing Rules

1. Free Core Utility

Finey provides core expense logging, visual charts, calendar breakdown, notes, local notifications, and CSV data export completely free of charge with zero mandatory subscription fees.

2. Digital Purchases & In-App Upgrades

If optional digital features, premium theme options, or developer tip contributions are offered within Finey, all payment transactions are processed exclusively through official app store distribution platforms (Google Play Billing and Apple App Store In-App Purchases).

3. Refund Request Procedures

Because financial transactions are handled directly by Google and Apple, refund processing is governed by store terms:

A. Google Play Store (Android)
  • Within 48 Hours: You can request a direct automated refund via your Google Play account purchase history (play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory).
  • After 48 Hours: Contact Finey support with your Google Play Order Number (GPA.XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXXX) for manual review.
B. Apple App Store (iOS)
  • Apple handles all payment processing and refunds directly. Finey developers do not possess authority to issue Apple App Store refunds directly.
  • To request a refund from Apple, visit reportaproblem.apple.com, log in with your Apple ID, select "I'd like to request a refund", and select your Finey purchase.

If you encounter technical issues with any digital purchase, please email us at:
