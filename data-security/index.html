Data Security & Encryption Architecture

Effective Date: August 1, 2026

1. Local Device Sandbox Protection

Finey utilizes Hive binary key-value storage engine isolated inside your Android / iOS application sandbox container. Other apps installed on your device cannot read Finey's database files without root/jailbreak overrides.

2. Zero Network Transmissions

No transactions, category entries, balance totals, or personal notes are transmitted across the internet. Finey contains zero background sync services or external API telemetry endpoints.

3. Data Ownership & Export

You own 100% of your financial data. You can export your data to CSV or JSON format at any time to back it up or transfer it to another tool.
